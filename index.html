    How it works
    1. Pick the meaning before you see it. Having to retrieve the answer is what makes it stick; just reading definitions does very little. If you don’t know, tap I’m not sure rather than guessing.
    2. Learnt = 5 correct in a row, on 5 different days. Five right answers in one sitting is short-term memory; the same five spread over days is what lasts.
    3. Gaps grow. After each correct day the word waits longer: 1, 2, 3, then 5 days. A miss resets the streak to 0, brings the word back later in the same session, and again tomorrow.
    4. Only scheduled answers count. Studying a level early or extra practice is fine, but a word only moves when its day has come.
    5. Learnt words still get check-ups after 14, 30 and 60 days. Miss one and it goes back to Learning.
    6. New words arrive from your sheet every other day. Add a word in column A of the Words tab and its meaning in column B.
