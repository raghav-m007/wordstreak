    How it works
    1. Recall first, then flip. Say the meaning in your head before you tap. Trying to pull it out of memory is what makes it stick; reading the answer does very little.
    2. Be strict. Tap Got it only if you had the meaning before flipping. Half-right is a miss.
    3. Learnt = 5 correct in a row, on 5 different days. Only your first answer each day counts. Five right answers in one sitting is short-term memory; the same five spread over days is what lasts.
    4. Gaps grow. After each correct day the word waits longer: 1, 2, 3, then 5 days. A miss resets the streak to 0, brings the word back later in the same session until you get it once, and again tomorrow.
    5. Learnt words still get check-ups after 14, 30 and 60 days. Miss one and it goes back to Learning.
    6. New words arrive from your sheet automatically every other day. Add a word in column A of the Words tab and its meaning in column B.
